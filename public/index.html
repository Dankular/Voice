Client-side VAD

Silero VAD running in your browser (ONNX Runtime Web). Audio never leaves the page. Captured speech segments can be exported as WAV, e.g. as a clean voice-cloning reference clip for OmniVoice-Onnx.

Voice design TTS

Pick attributes, get a generated voice from OmniVoice (voice design, no reference audio). Unset filters are left for the model to choose. There is no documented emotion control: "tone" here means pitch, plus the whisper style.
